Nessie

I designed the climb mechanism for the 2025 robot. The objective was to have a robot hang three inches off the ground via a cage, so we opted for a butterfly-like design that pushes against the cage to allow the robot to lift itself off the ground.

CAD of the climb mechanism | Everything was fabricated in house using 6061 aluminum and PLA
